Splatterhop™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Splatterhop™ is a puzzle game for iPhone made by SSA Digital LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Who we are

SSA Digital LLC, 4101 Dublin Blvd Ste F #2002, Dublin, CA 94568, USA. Email: support@ssadigital.biz. Phone: (925) 264-9280. We are the only operator of the game.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (levels, stars, daily puzzle streak, hints and settings) is stored only on your device.

The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year before any ad is requested. We use it only for legal compliance and player safety (for example, players under 13 are never shown Google’s privacy form), then keep just an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) on your device. The year itself is not stored or sent anywhere. If the answer is skipped or unclear, the game treats the player as under 13. Where the law requires it (for example in Texas, Utah and Louisiana), the game also asks Apple for your age range (such as “13 to 15”), which you or your parent choose whether to share. Apple’s answer is used only for legal compliance and player safety, only while the game is open, and is never saved; the game asks again the next time it opens. If the answers differ, the younger age group is used, and if Apple’s check is required but no age is shared, the player is treated as under 13.

If you turn on daily puzzle reminders, each reminder is scheduled on your phone by iOS. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off in the game’s Settings.

After a win, the game may show Apple’s own request to rate the app; Apple decides how often it appears. It collects nothing.

The game does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Splatterhop™ is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for a hint or to skip a level, and occasional ads between levels.

  • Every player, of any age, is treated the way the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires for children. Every ad request is marked as child-directed, no ads are personalized, and the game never asks anyone for tracking permission or uses the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • All ads are G-rated (general audience) for every player, matching the game’s 4+ App Store age rating.
  • Sensitive ad categories such as dating, gambling, alcohol and politics are blocked for everyone.

Even though ads are not personalized, Google uses device information to show ads in context, limit how often you see the same ad, count views and clicks, and prevent fraud. For every player, device identifiers are used only for these internal operations, as COPPA allows, and never to build a profile or show personalized ads. Our safeguards: every ad request is tagged as child-directed, which tells Google to turn off personalized ads and remarketing for it; the game never asks for tracking permission or reads the advertising identifier; and we test this in every release before it ships. We follow a written security plan, reviewed every year, that covers this data. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information (such as an advertising or device identifier), approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data. Google, not us, receives this information, and it may be processed in the United States.

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK), players aged 13 and over may be shown Google’s privacy form and can change their choice later in the game’s Settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children and parents

Splatterhop™ is made for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we never use children’s information for personalized advertising or tracking. All ads are child-directed, non-personalized and G-rated, for every player. Nobody sees the tracking prompt.

Our only partners who may receive device information are Google (AdMob, for the ads described above) and Apple (purchases and age range, when used). We do not sell or rent anyone’s information.

Parents can contact us at any time using the details above. Because we hold no player data ourselves, parents can delete all of a child’s game data, and stop any further collection, by deleting the app from the device.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not keep any player data ourselves: game data stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes it, and we have no copies on any server. The birth year is discarded as soon as the age group is chosen. If you email us for support or to report an ad (Settings › Report an ad, behind a grown-up check), we keep that email for up to 12 months after your question is resolved, then delete it. If an email comes from a child, we reply once, then delete it, and we never use it to contact the sender again. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention rules (see the link above). To ask Google about advertising data, see the link above.

Security

The game keeps no personal data on servers, so there is no player database to protect. Our developer accounts use two-factor sign-in, and we review this policy and our partners at least once a year and before adding any new data use.

California residents

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising: no player is tracked and no ads are personalized. You may ask us what information we hold (none from the game; only any support emails you sent us) or to delete it by emailing us; we will not treat you differently for asking.

Do Not Track: the game has no web browsing and does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. It does not track players across other apps or websites, and it does not allow Google to do so for personalized ads.

United Kingdom

We rely on legitimate interests to show contextual, non-personalized ads that keep the game free; the game does not show personalized ads. You have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your information, and to change any privacy choice at any time in the game’s Settings.
