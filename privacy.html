Valet Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Valet Rush is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. We do not run our own servers. Your game progress (score, coins, levels and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Valet Rush is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Information about ads shown and how you interact with them
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information

If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Players in the EU, UK and similar regions are shown a consent form, and can change their choice later in the game under Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores are shared with Apple to show leaderboards. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Valet Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset progress in the game, or delete the app, to remove all game data from your device. To ask Google about advertising data, see the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
