A tiny snake on a big rescue

Lost ducks, bunnies and piglets are scattered across busy roads and rivers. Hop your little snake from lane to lane, pick them up one by one, and bring the whole wobbly parade safely home to its nest. The more critters you bring home at once, the bigger the celebration.

What's inside

  • One-tap hopping: easy to pick up, hard to put down
  • Dodge rollers, cross rivers and watch out for the hawk
  • Unlock 6 silly snakes and collect stickers along the road
  • Daily challenges, free gifts and a Lucky Wheel
  • Plays offline, no account needed
