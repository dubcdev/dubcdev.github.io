Snake Parade™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Snake Parade™ is a game for iPhone made by SSA Digital LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Who we are

SSA Digital LLC, 4101 Dublin Blvd Ste F #2002, Dublin, CA 94568, USA. Email: support@ssadigital.biz. Phone: (925) 264-9280. We are the only operator of the game.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (critters rescued, stickers, apples, missions and settings) is stored only on your device.

The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year before any ad is requested. We use it only to decide which kind of ads to show, then keep just an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) on your device. The year itself is not stored or sent anywhere. Where the law requires it (for example in Texas, Utah and Louisiana), the game may instead ask Apple for your age range (such as “13 to 15”), which you or your parent choose whether to share. We use it only for the same purpose and keep only the age group. If the two answers differ, the younger age group is used. Where Apple’s age check is required, a player who chooses not to share an age range is treated as under 13.

If you turn on gift reminders, the reminder is scheduled on your phone by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

The game does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snake Parade is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch (for example to double your apples or keep a run going), and occasional ads between runs.

To protect every player, we handle all players’ ad data the way U.S. children’s privacy law (COPPA) requires for children under 13, whatever their age:

  • Every ad request is marked for Google’s child-directed treatment, and ads are never personalized.
  • No one is asked for tracking permission, and the game never tracks anyone across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Ad content depends on age: players under 13 see only G-rated ads; players 13 and over may see ads rated up to Teen. Sensitive categories (such as dating, gambling and alcohol) are blocked for everyone.

Even though ads are not personalized, Google uses device information only for internal operations that COPPA allows: showing ads in context, limiting how often you see the same ad, counting views and clicks, and preventing fraud. Device identifiers are never used to build a profile of anyone. To do this, Google may collect device information (such as a device identifier), approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data. Google, not us, receives this information, and it may be processed in the United States.

Players in the UK and similar regions are shown Google’s consent form and can change their choice later in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores are shared with Apple to show leaderboards. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children and parents

Snake Parade is made for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we never use children’s information for personalized advertising or tracking. Ads shown to players under 13 are child-directed, G-rated and non-personalized, and no player of any age gets personalized ads or a tracking prompt.

Our only partners who may receive device information are Google (AdMob, for the ads described above) and Apple (Game Center, purchases and age range, when used). We do not sell or rent anyone’s information.

Parents can contact us at any time using the details above. Because we hold no player data ourselves, parents can delete all of a child’s game data, and stop any further collection, by deleting the app from the device.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not keep any player data ourselves: game data stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes it, and we have no copies on any server. The birth year is discarded as soon as the age group is chosen. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention rules (see the link above), and Game Center data by Apple. To ask Google about advertising data, see the link above.

Security

The game keeps no personal data on servers, so there is no player database to protect. Our developer accounts use two-factor sign-in, and we review this policy and our partners at least once a year and before adding any new data use.

California residents

We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, for players of any age. You may ask us what information we hold (none) or to delete it by emailing us; we will not treat you differently for asking.

Do Not Track: the game has no web browsing and does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. It does not track anyone across other companies’ apps or websites in the first place.

United Kingdom

SSA Digital LLC is the controller for the age group stored on your device. Google is an independent controller for the advertising data it collects. We rely on legitimate interests to show contextual, non-personalized ads that keep the game free; the game never shows personalized ads. Where the law requires consent for storing or reading information on your device, Google’s consent form asks for it. You have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your information, and to withdraw consent at any time in the game’s Settings. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the game.
