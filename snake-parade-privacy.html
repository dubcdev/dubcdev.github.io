Snake Parade Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Snake Parade is a game for iPhone made by SSA Digital LLC (“we”). This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (critters rescued, stickers, apples, missions and settings) is stored only on your device.

The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year. We use it only to decide which kind of ads to show, then keep just an age group (under 13, or 13 and over) on your device. The year itself is not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snake Parade is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.

  • Players under 13 see only ads marked as child-directed and non-personalized, under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The game does not ask them for tracking permission.
  • Players 13 and over may be asked by Apple’s tracking prompt whether ads can use the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information, approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data. Players in the EU, UK and similar regions are shown a consent form and can change their choice later in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores are shared with Apple to show leaderboards. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Snake Parade is made for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads shown to players who say they are under 13 are child-directed and non-personalized.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes it. We keep no copies. To ask Google about advertising data, see the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
