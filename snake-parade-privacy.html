Snake Parade Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Snake Parade is a game for iPhone made by SSA Digital LLC (“we”). This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (critters rescued, stickers, apples, missions and settings) is stored only on your device.

The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year. We use it only to decide which kind of ads to show, then keep just an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) on your device. The year itself is not stored or sent anywhere. Where the law requires it (for example in Texas, Utah and Louisiana), the game may instead ask Apple for your age range (such as “13 to 15”), which you or your parent choose whether to share. We use it only for the same purpose and keep only the age group.

If you turn on gift reminders, the reminder is scheduled on your phone by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snake Parade is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.

  • Players under 13 see only ads marked as child-directed and non-personalized, under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The game does not ask them for tracking permission.
  • Players 13 to 17 see only non-personalized ads and are never asked for tracking permission.
  • Players 18 and over may be asked by Apple’s tracking prompt whether ads can use the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Even when ads are not personalized, Google uses device information to show ads in context, limit how often you see the same ad, count views and clicks, and prevent fraud (for players under 13 this is limited to these internal operations, as COPPA allows). To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information, approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data. Players in the EU, UK and similar regions are shown a consent form and can change their choice later in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores are shared with Apple to show leaderboards. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Snake Parade is made for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we never use children’s information for personalized advertising or tracking. Ads shown to players who say they are under 13 are child-directed, G-rated and non-personalized; players under 18 never get personalized ads. Our only partners who may receive device information are Google (AdMob, for the ads described above) and Apple (Game Center, purchases and age range, when used). Parents can contact us at the address below with any question, and can delete all game data at any time by deleting the app.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not keep any player data ourselves: game data stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes it, and we have no copies on any server. The birth year is discarded as soon as the age group is chosen. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention rules (see the link above), and Game Center data by Apple. To ask Google about advertising data, see the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
