A puzzle with wet paint

Hop Dabble across the board, and every tile you land on gets painted. The catch? Fresh paint is wet, so you can never land on the same tile twice. Paint the whole board without getting stuck. Every level is its own little brain-teaser: plan your route, think a few hops ahead, and feel the click when it all fits.

What's inside

  • 120 levels across four worlds: Meadow, Paint Pots, Ice Rink and Stormy
  • Two-coat tiles, cracked planks, paint pots, slippery ice and Grump the storm cloud
  • A fresh daily puzzle with a streak to protect
  • Three stars per level: paint it all, beat the hop goal, and do it without Undo
  • Plays offline, no account needed
